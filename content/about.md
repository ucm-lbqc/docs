The **Laboratorio de Bioinformática y Química Computacional (LBQC)** is a research laboratory at [Universidad Católica del Maule](https://www.ucm.cl) (UCM) in Talca, Chile.
It belongs to the [Departamento de Medicina Traslacional](https://www.ucm.cl/facultades/facultad-de-medicina/departamentos/), Facultad de Medicina.
Several members are also researchers at the [Centro de Investigación de Estudios Avanzados del Maule (CIEAM)](https://www.ucm.cl/ciencia-e-investigacion/comunidad-creacion-y-desarrollo/centros-de-investigacion/cieam/).

The laboratory was established in 2022.
It supports research and teaching in **computational chemistry**, **molecular simulation**, and **bioinformatics** (including genomics workflows on the shared cluster).

## Location

Laboratory **C111**, Sector C, San Miguel campus, Talca, Chile.

The same building area holds a classroom for teaching and meetings, a storage room, and the cluster server room.

## Infrastructure

- Teaching and meeting classroom
- Server room (login node and compute nodes)
- Storage room
- Shared HPC cluster documented under [Docs]({{< relref "docs" >}})

Cluster hardware was acquired with support from the Faculty of Medicine, UCM, and ANID (SIA SA772100091), Chile.
