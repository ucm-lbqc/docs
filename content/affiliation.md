Use these strings in manuscripts, posters, and theses when the work was done in the LBQC.
Copy the language of the rest of the paper (Spanish or English).
If a co-author has a second appointment (CIEAM, oncology centre, another university), add that as an extra affiliation, not instead of the laboratory.

## Laboratory (use this)

**English**

> Computational Bioinformatics and Chemistry Laboratory (LBQC), Department of Translational Medicine, Faculty of Medicine, Universidad Católica del Maule, Talca, Chile

**Spanish**

> Laboratorio de Bioinformática y Química Computacional (LBQC), Departamento de Medicina Traslacional, Facultad de Medicina, Universidad Católica del Maule, Talca, Chile

City and postcode when a journal asks for them: Talca 3480094, Chile.
Campus: San Miguel.

## Second affiliation (when it applies)

Some members are also at CIEAM. If that is your appointment, add a **second** affiliation:

**English**

> Centro de Investigación de Estudios Avanzados del Maule (CIEAM), Universidad Católica del Maule, Talca, Chile

**Spanish**

> Centro de Investigación de Estudios Avanzados del Maule (CIEAM), Universidad Católica del Maule, Talca, Chile

Do not replace the LBQC line with CIEAM alone if the work used the laboratory or the cluster.

External collaborators keep their home institution as their primary affiliation and may add the LBQC line if the work was performed here.

## Acknowledgements (cluster)

If the job ran on the LBQC cluster, mention it and the funding, for example:

> Computations were performed on the LBQC cluster at Universidad Católica del Maule. Hardware was funded by the Faculty of Medicine, UCM, and ANID (SIA SA772100091), Chile.
